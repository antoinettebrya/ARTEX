# Content evidence and editorial rules

The content engine consumes the market's stored keyword, natural SERP snapshot, page mapping, opportunity score and competitor structure. It joins those signals with the project site's captured pages. Usable strategies retain their evidence IDs and explain the recommended URL and page type. A canonical conflict, unverified target, or empty inventory may hold the strategy. Two competing URLs can support consolidation. Duplicate text and model review are separate signals from the recorded search positions.

Briefs are built per project and market. Each of English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Thai, Burmese and Khmer has independent editorial guidance. Secondary terms come from its Phase 2 keyword cluster where available. Competitor inputs contain bounded theme and entity labels and recorded gaps. Internal link recommendations refer to captured, related URLs in the project scope. The brief lists source URLs and fact-check requirements to guide writing.

Facts are whole short assertions from captured sources marked official, government, primary or authorized. A candidate records the exact source ID and supported excerpt. One approved official, government or primary source can verify an assertion. An authorized source alone yields partial evidence. Two independently hosted current authorized sources can corroborate it. The engine groups statements whose numeric values or explicit negation differ under an otherwise matching frame. It retains both excerpts and blocks unresolved conflicts. Evidence is refreshed after six months. Time-sensitive assertions also consider the source's published date where available.

Fact checking compares declared claims and detectable material assertions against rendered title, description, H1, headings and body. A cited fact ID only verifies matching wording in the stored evidence, after conservative punctuation and presentation normalization. Ambiguous paraphrases remain unresolved. Scenario instructions whose structure has no detectable quantitative, temporal, legal, policy or attribution claim can receive separate editorial review. The engine recognizes these categories through writing declarations, localized cues and Unicode numerals. An independent reviewer remains necessary for naturalness, intent and new reader value.

The versioned quality gate assigns points in twelve dimensions and reports weighted points, raw metrics, issues, suggested changes and the review time. A score of at least 75 and passing independent review are required. Missing market evidence, unsupported material facts, expired or missing citations, unsafe markup, duplicate content, language mismatch, broken heading structure or invalid SEO metadata block publication preparation. Duplicate detection uses ordered text shingles and topic vocabulary against other captured pages in the same site and language.

SEO optimization prepares concise metadata, hierarchy, canonical and slug suggestions, relevant internal links, image alt suggestions and article schema while preserving authored body and citations. Refresh recommendations anchor to the original inventory snapshot and point to recorded performance or source evidence when provided. Recommendations may preserve, edit, rewrite, consolidate or recommend review of noindex.
